Tunerrific Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

Tunerrific is a free musical instrument tuning and practice application. It contains no advertisements, analytics or tracking technologies.

1. Microphone and Audio Processing

Tunerrific uses microphone access to detect musical pitch while the tuner is active. Audio is processed locally on your device in real time. The app does not record, save, upload or transmit microphone audio.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Tunerrific stores app preferences and functionality data locally on your device. This may include instrument and tuning selections, custom tunings, app settings, metronome tempo, practice-game scores and onboarding status. This information is not transmitted to Tunerrific or third parties by the app.

3. Data Collection and Sharing

Tunerrific does not collect personal information, operate user accounts, display advertisements or use analytics or tracking services. The app does not share user data with third parties.

4. Permissions

  • Microphone: Used for real-time pitch detection.
  • Vibration: Used for optional haptic feedback.

The app declares an internet permission, but its current functionality does not use the network to transmit user data.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

App preferences and settings are stored locally. Uninstalling Tunerrific generally removes its local app data. Automatic Android app backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled by the app's configuration.

6. Children's Privacy

Tunerrific does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.
